Needs Olive Tree Bible Study installed on the device that opens this. Tapping a reading hands off straight to that passage in the app — to the exact starting verse when a reading begins mid-chapter, or to the top of the chapter for a full-chapter read.

Reading range notation: when an assignment runs to the last verse of a chapter, the endpoint is shown as a bare chapter number (or "end") instead of a verse number — e.g. "1:12–2" means chapter 1 verse 12 through the end of chapter 2, and "2:5–end" means chapter 2 verse 5 through the end of that chapter. A verse number is only shown when the reading actually stops mid-chapter. Chapter numbers are shown in bold, so in "1:12–2" the 2 is a chapter, while in "1:1–25" the 25 is a verse.
